About the Artist

Siyu, an artist and designer from Beijing, China, is currently studying dual degrees in Art & Design and UI/UX, with a minor in Business, at the University of Michigan.

Her multidisciplinary practice spans oil painting, illustration, and AR/VR, reflecting her curiosity about the convergence of traditional and digital media. Inspired by themes of surrealism, magical realism, and Daoist philosophy, Siyu’s work explores liminal spaces and the tension between the real and the imagined. She constructs ethereal, dreamlike visuals that reimagine natural subjects in abstract forms, inviting audiences to question their perception of reality.

Through her art, Siyu seeks to evoke a sense of "otherworldly beauty," offering viewers a transcendent experience that resonates with the unconscious mind. Her creative vision bridges the gap between familiar and fantastical, forging a deeper connection with audiences through immersive, thought-provoking worlds.
