About the Artist

Siyu, an artist and designer from Beijing, China, is currently studying dual degrees in Art & Design and UI/UX, with a minor in Business, at the University of Michigan.

Through her art, Siyu seeks to evoke a sense of "otherworldly beauty," offering viewers a transcendent experience that resonates with the unconscious mind. Her creative vision bridges the gap between familiar and fantastical, forging a deeper connection with audiences through immersive, thought-provoking worlds.
